# Brand guidelines

## Brand idea

Present Afghanistan through trust, hospitality, cultural depth, natural beauty and carefully guided discovery. The design must feel premium and authentic, not militarized or like a generic booking marketplace.

## Core colors

| Role      | Name               | Hex       | Meaning                                                     |
| --------- | ------------------ | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary   | Lapis Navy         | `#163B5C` | Trust, strength, mountains and Afghanistan’s lapis heritage |
| Secondary | Heritage Turquoise | `#0E7373` | Cultural tilework, discovery and Band-e Amir                |
| CTA       | Saffron Gold       | `#D7A23A` | Hospitality, warmth, craftsmanship and premium service      |

## 60/30/10 application

- 60% Lapis family for structure, headings, navigation, footer and dominant framing.
- 30% Turquoise family for active states, links, filters, secondary actions, icons and informational panels.
- 10% Gold for the primary CTA and limited premium emphasis.
- Functional neutrals do not compete with this ratio.
- Never use all three colors at equal visual weight.
- Usually only one Gold primary action should appear in a viewport.

## Typography

- English headings: Manrope
- English body/UI: Inter
- Dari/Pashto: Noto Sans Arabic
- Use readable line length and comfortable line height.
- Avoid excessive uppercase text.

## Visual character

- Premium
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Respectful
- Photographic
- Adventure-oriented
- Authentic

## Photography

- Use real landscapes, architecture, food, crafts, guides and respectful human moments.
- Avoid conflict stereotypes.
- Maintain consistent aspect ratios.
- Use meaningful alt text.
- Add proper scrims behind text overlays.

## Theme principle

- Light theme: airy, editorial and welcoming.
- Dark theme: cinematic, premium and calm.
- Do not use pure black or neon colors.
